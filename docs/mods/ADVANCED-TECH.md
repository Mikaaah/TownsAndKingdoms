# Advanced Powered Technology — Evaluation

T&K3 should not remain rotational-only. The intended curve is:

**Create mechanical industry → electrification → advanced powered machines/chemistry → AE2-backed logistics/autocrafting**

## Primary prototype

### Mekanism + Mekanism Generators
Role: Chapters V–VIII advanced technology.

Strengths:
- FE-powered machine progression
- gases/chemistry and processing methods Create cannot naturally express
- scalable factories and late-game energy
- strong candidate for advanced metallurgy/material processing

Controls:
- do not include Mekanism Tools by default
- Digital Miner must be late-gated, reworked or disabled if it bypasses exploration
- deep ore multiplication must not trivialize mining/material economy
- Mekasuit/late equipment must not replace T&K's curated RPG equipment loop
- nuclear/fusion power belongs late

## Transition layer

### Create Crafts & Additions
Preferred bridge from rotation to FE:
- early electrical generation
- converts existing Create infrastructure into the first meaningful power network
- avoids introducing a separate full tech tree just to explain electricity

## Alternatives / specialist candidates

### Immersive Engineering
Best for visible industrial electrification, wires, Arc Furnace, refinery and multiblocks.
Risk: overlaps heavily with Create and may not feel advanced enough as the final tech layer.

### PneumaticCraft: Repressurized
Excellent unique processing through pressure, heat, refinery, PCB-style production and drones.
Risk: adds another energy system and can become its own parallel tech tree.

### Ender IO
Useful compact powered machines, alloys and conduits.
Risk: 1.21.1 is beta and conduits overlap AE2/Create logistics.

### Industrial Foregoing
Strong specialized automation for farming, mobs and resources.
Risk: can trivialize kingdom/resource loops very quickly.

### Thermal Series
Do not use as a foundation target for now. There is no official 1.21.1 Thermal release; unofficial ports are too new to make a core dependency.

## Current preferred architecture

Chapters I–IV:
- Create = physical/mechanical manufacturing

Chapter IV/V:
- Create Crafts & Additions = electrification bridge

Chapters V–VIII:
- Mekanism = advanced powered processing, chemistry and energy

Chapter V onward:
- AE2 = storage, logistics, autocrafting and factory control across both Create and powered tech

This keeps each technology layer responsible for a distinct capability instead of making three mods perform the same recipes.
