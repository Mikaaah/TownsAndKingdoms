## Conditional / archive

- Crushed Raw Cobalt: only keep if cobalt earns a role in the final material roster.
- Boot Medium and unused placeholders: archive unless a concrete system needs them.
- Quest/icon textures remain available for quest-book presentation but are not progression resources.

## New texture budget for Chapters I–V

Target **0–3 genuinely new base resource textures**. Prefer existing assets, recolours, overlays and mod-provided materials. Save new art time for signature weapons, bosses and truly unique endgame resources.
