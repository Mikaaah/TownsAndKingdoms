# Equipment Manufacturing

Weapons and armor are part of industrial progression.

## Component families
blade/head, guard, handle/haft, grip, binding, pommel, armor plate, chain, padding, bow limb/string, magical core/focus

## Typical flow
ore → refined metal → plate/billet → blank → forging/shaping → grinding/tempering → component → sequenced assembly → equipment

## Rules
- weapon variety must justify itself through gameplay
- remove/hide redundant near-identical equipment
- boss materials alter components/processes, not just 3x3 ingredients
- higher tiers introduce capabilities, not only larger costs
