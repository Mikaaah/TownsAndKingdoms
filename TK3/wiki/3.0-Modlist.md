## Pack direction

T&K3 is being built around connected progression rather than independent mod islands:

- Create = early/mid mechanical processing
- Mekanism = later technology
- AE2 = late-game logistics/autocrafting
- Iron's Spells = combat magic
- Ars Nouveau = arcane crafting/automation
- FTB Quests = visible progression
- AStages = hard gameplay gates
- Bosses/milestones = progression permissions and reusable processing catalysts
- Epic Fight = combat framework with T&K3-maintained compatibility

The goal is not to maximize mod count. Every selected mod should have a clear role in progression, worldbuilding, integration or multiplayer.
