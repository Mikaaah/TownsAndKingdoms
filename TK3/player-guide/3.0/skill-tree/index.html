Classes & professions

One giant skill tree connects your class, subclass, general abilities and profession branches. The current design offers six classes and eighteen subclasses. Choose one main class and one subclass to define your character.

Professions

Mining, Logging, Farming and Fishing have their own branches and mastery paths. Each profession has six focus nodes; choose at most three. Profession branches remain separate from class branches.

Character growth

Paths combine general improvements with class-specific choices. Percentage effects and flat statistics are presented consistently so you can compare nodes clearly.

Current status

This describes the v3.2.2 design. The character tree is still being developed separately from the chapter 1–5 recipe package.
