Project reference

Mods, addons & tiers

See each mod’s workshop access, role and selection status across the ten-chapter campaign.

ONE CAMPAIGN · TEN WORKSHOPS · CONNECTED SYSTEMS.

This map follows the current selected mod list and every addon / support entry. A tier means access to the relevant workshop or encounter branch; terrain, UI mods and required libraries keep native roles. Native rituals, boss seals, research, channels, power consumption and flight physics still matter.

The ten-tier route

Chapter Workshop Main systems
1 Roots of Industry Algal binder, rotation and your first workshop
2 Copperworks Sealed assembly, tanks and fluid handling
3 Brass & Precision Heated metallurgy, deterministic assembly and organised logistics
4 Arcane Industry Living source, haunting and the Wilden trial
5 The Industrial Bridge Steel, FE and basic Mekanism processing
6 The Connected Kingdom AE2 networks, processing patterns and living logistics
7 Beyond the Horizon Airships, chemical systems and purposeful exploration
8 Containment & Control Fission, advanced storage and safe nuclear production
9 Singularity Engineering End access, fusion and antimatter without a grind wall
10 The Sovereign Project Join every workshop into a renewable kingdom
