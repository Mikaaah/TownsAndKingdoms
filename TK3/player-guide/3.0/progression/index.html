T&K3 — Progression & Recipes

Open the full player guide

Start with a small manual workshop, automate materials with Create, connect Source to machinery and build your first FE network.

Your first workshop

  1. Gather kelp, clay and andesite.
  2. Craft Algal Blend, then Andesite Alloy.
  3. Craft the first Kinetic Machine from seven Andesite Alloy, one wooden slab and one Andesite Casing (AAA / ACA / ASA).
  4. Use a stonecutter to turn the frame into the machine you need.
  5. Add rotational power, a press, basin, mixer and deployer to improve efficiency and automate further frames.

A frame is consumed when stonecut. One frame gives the selected recipe output: for example three water wheels, two basins or one press. You do not receive every machine at once.

Chapters

Chapter Frame / milestone Main production layer
I · Roots of Industry Kinetic Machine Binder, alloy, rotation and a basic workshop
II · Copperworks Hydraulic Machine Sealant and fluid handling
III · Brass & Precision Precision Machine Brass, deterministic assembly and smart logistics
IV · Arcane Industry Arcane Machine / Wilden trial Source, essences and magical production
V · Industrial Bridge Steel Casing / first FE Power, Infusers and refining

Complete the chapter milestones to unlock later machines. Optional quests introduce useful side systems. Item tasks check possession; factory checkmarks are your confirmation that the setup works.

Mechanism assembly and permanent tools

Mechanism Ordered belt/depot operations Final held tool
Kinetic Wooden slab → alloy → second alloy BetterEnd Iron Hammer
Sealed Kinetic Mechanism → copper sheet → slimeball Farmer’s Delight Iron Knife
Precision Sealed Mechanism → brass sheet → Electron Tube Create Sand Paper
Arcane Precision Mechanism → Source Gem → Arcane Essence → Manipulation Essence → gold Ars Enchanter’s Sword

The final operation uses a deployer holding the tool. Every sequence has one loop and a guaranteed output. Ordinary tools lose one durability. Chapters I–IV award unbreakable versions once per player; chapter V awards a second permanent Iron Hammer for a parallel line. Make the ordinary tools first; the reward removes later maintenance.

Automated frames use casing → deploy one mechanism: Andesite/Kinetic, Copper/Sealed, Brass/Precision and Wizardry Arcane/Arcane. The Arcane Casing uses an apparatus recipe for a Brass Casing with Source Gem, Arcane Essence and gold, costing 500 Source. Every mechanism requires sequenced assembly. Only the first Kinetic Machine keeps a more expensive raw-material crafting recipe. Stonecutting still selects the workshop machine.

Renewable resources

Build a normal lava/water generator. Under the generated stone, place a selector material (the lens), then the required machine frame beneath it. The lens and frame stay in place while a drill collects the stone.

Each stone has a distinct yield: andesite/clay, diorite/quartz, granite/lapis, limestone/bone meal, scoria/redstone, scorchia/coal, veridium/copper, crimsite/iron, asurine/zinc and ochrum/gold.

Quick answers

  • Slimeballs: kelp + wheat + 250 mB water in a mixer → two slimeballs. Use a bucket before you have a pump.
  • First Source: Source Jar and a native Volcanic/Vitalic Sourcelink after chapter III, before your custom Agronomic Sourcelink.
  • Wilden Tribute: the Infuser recipe retains the held catalyst.
  • First steel: two iron ingots + coal in a heated mixer → two steel ingots, without FE.
  • AE2: follows in later chapters, after the first industrial bridge.
