Towns & multiplayer

Build settlements, manage shared supplies, protect your home and connect your group’s workshops.

BUILD A HOME THAT HAS A JOB TO DO.

MineColonies is T&K3's selected settlement system. Build your town alongside the workshop that supplies it, and organise a shared home with the selected multiplayer tools.

MineColonies · your settlement

MineColonies is the foundation for colony building, citizens and settlement management. Plan space for the town as well as the factory: clear routes and organised supply areas make expansion easier to follow.

The selected Create: MineColonies Link connects colony supply requests to Create logistics. This gives your production lines another purpose as the town grows.

A practical layout

Area Give it a clear purpose
Town centre Settlement management and everyday access
Workshop Renewable supplies and material processing
Supply storage A clear handover point for colony resources
Travel point A convenient place to leave and return
Expansion space Room for additional buildings and production lines

Keep busy machine routes and frequently used walkways easy to distinguish. Label storage by what it supplies so teammates can help without having to learn every recipe chain first.

Teams & claims

FTB Teams is the selected group system and FTB Chunks is the selected claim system. Agree on your team's building area, shared storage and responsibilities before expanding a communal settlement.

Use the server's actual claim and chunk-loading rules when planning a large colony or factory.

Travel & shared loot

Waystones connects travel destinations. Lootr handles normal multiplayer structure loot in the selected stack; progression-critical rewards follow T&K3's progression rules.

Create Aeronautics is also selected for the engineering and travel layer, with its multiplayer integrations still being developed.
