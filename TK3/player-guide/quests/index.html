Field guide/The questbook

The questbook

Use the book to find your next project, learn a system and track your progress. The main production route connects your workshop; side paths give room for exploration, magic, building and community projects.

The questbook is being rebuilt. Current recipe chapters are documented here, while quest totals, final rewards and completion commands await a complete matching quest export. The target is at least 1,500 useful quests; that is a design goal.

Adventure pages

Exploration gathers Alex's Caves, Twilight Forest, Aether and Deeper and Darker. Bosses is the encounter/bounty direction. The Eyes follows End Remastered.

Native eye acquisition still applies: the custom boss/chest mapping script currently has no added assignments. Boss rematches and any quest-token price/timer need their final configured reward route; this wiki does not assign a guessed altar command.

Ponder from a quest

Hover a supported item in a task and hold W. The 97-lesson reference includes stone generation, processors, mechanisms, industrial multiblocks and Workshop features.

What the old export contains

The supplied base export contains many inherited TK2 chapters and items, including old space and magic labels. They are migration material. The wiki does not present those records as a finished 1,240-quest TNK3 campaign or restore the old eleven-chapter design.
