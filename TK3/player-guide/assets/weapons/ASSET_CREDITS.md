# Weapon catalogue artwork

Towns & Kingdoms branding uses the existing pack-owned field-guide artwork.

Nine unmodified item sprites come from Thaumaturge Tinker 1.1.2 (Minecraft
1.21.1, CurseForge project 1792741 / file 9648983), copyright (c) 2026 Kajitsy.
They are distributed under the MIT License; see `THAUMATURGE_TINKER_MIT.txt`.
Source: https://github.com/Kajitsy/Thaumaturge-Tinkers

Other catalogue art uses category markers rather than copying restricted mod
textures. Markers identify a weapon category and do not represent its model.
English item labels come from the installed mods' language data where available;
other labels are readable registry names. Every entry includes its exact ID.
