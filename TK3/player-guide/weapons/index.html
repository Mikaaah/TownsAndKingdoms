Read the stats

Damage, speed, impact and extra-target figures are native capability bonuses. They add to the item and preset attributes; they are not final damage or DPS. Armor-negation bonuses use percentage points. Hand-specific values take priority over common values. A dash or “Preset” means the capability inherits that field.

Base item damage and attack speed are shown separately where verified: all 133 Simply Swords weapons and five Thaumaturge Tinker weapons. Skills, animations, active abilities, enchantments and character progression can change the result in play.

WoM weapon selection

The six native staffs, four greataxes and Nova are retained. Agony and Ruine are provisionally retained. The other 14 original WoM weapons are excluded from recipes and loot; enable “Show excluded” to inspect their mappings. Other weapons still use their assigned WoM and WoM Plus movesets.

Catalogue reference

The catalogue contains 646 entries: 514 non-excluded weapons, 20 shields, 88 mapped tools, ten utility/casting items and 14 excluded WoM items. It combines 633 explicit capability mappings, ten verified automatic mappings and three registered items whose combat style remains unverified. Knaves’ Needs lists only the pack’s Twilight Forest weapons. Those gaps are marked “No explicit mapping” rather than assigned invented types or stats.

This is a dated static reference, not a live registry dump. Being listed does not guarantee an item has a crafting or loot route.
