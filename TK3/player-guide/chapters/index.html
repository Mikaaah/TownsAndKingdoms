Chapters & quests

All 115 quests across ten chapters, with goals, dependencies and permanent workshop rewards.

Main quests follow the workshop spine. Optional branches introduce side systems. Item goals are detected in game; setup checkmarks are self-reported. This is the authored campaign, currently in development.
