Field guide/Find recipes faster

Find recipes faster

JEI is the current lookup for the installed recipe set. The wiki explains the production route; the game resolves enabled mods, tags, conditions and dynamic spell recipes.

Nineteen useful searches

These are ordinary item-tag searches, not new JEI tabs. Type the exact query, including #.

Read the information

Information pages explain processor dies, ore yields, rubber heating, scroll filters, paintballs, Mana, Creation items and other non-obvious behavior. The Wrench and Compass also carry the search-query reference.

Short tooltips identify reusable presses and important tool/fluid requirements. An ingredient's internal ID can retain an older name while its visible name has changed.

Ponder

Hover a supported item and hold W, or your rebound Ponder key. You can do this directly over an item task in the questbook.

The pack provides 97 authored lessons in the current collection, alongside native lessons supplied by compatible mods. Search the lesson index.

When an item is hidden

Some entries are compatibility remnants or developer-only items; others are fully disabled for the pack. Hidden-only entries and disabled gameplay are different. The balance guide explains the selected removals.
