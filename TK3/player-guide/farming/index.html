Field guide/A home worth returning to

A home worth returning to

The factory supports a comfortable base. Build a kitchen, keep gardens close to storage, and turn exploration's palettes into places you enjoy using.

Grow variety

Farmer's Delight and Croptopia are part of the selected farming family, alongside Veggies Delight, Corn Delight, fruit/food addons and the Let's Do collection. Use their installed recipe links and cutting-board processing rather than assuming ingredients with similar names are interchangeable.

Spice of Life: Carrot Edition rewards trying different foods. Beta 1.0 sets milestones at 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 unique foods, and keeps the recorded food list after death.

The pack disables the confirmed reusable foods Everlasting Beef, Eternal Steak and conditional Infinity Ham. Normal meals, Farmer's Delight ham and smoked ham remain ordinary food options.

Connect the kitchen

Create farms, collection and processing can feed storage and food production. Slice & Dice and compatible integrations connect selected recipes. Preserve a small manual supply while automating crops with longer growth or unusual harvest behavior.

Origins and activities distinguish hand harvesting from machine production. A farm can be useful without every machine-harvested crop earning a player's hand-harvest bonus.

Materials from the environment

Biomes O' Plenty and Regions Unexplored fibers have hand, Cutting Board and Saw alternatives where configured. Moss can become string and reeds can become paper. These are practical local-material uses, without making every household craft cost a mechanism.

Building automation includes selected BetterEnd, Quark and other materials. Chipped-style workstations, Lili's Pottery and decorative palettes keep their own roles. Copycats+, Diagonal Fences, Etched and Torchmaster are included; Construction Sticks joins Workshop's building tools.

Build a town your way

Use shared storage, public workshops, farms, roads and a Trading Hub. Town building in Beta 1.0 centres on player-built settlements; MineColonies is part of the preserved TK2 setup.
