Sovereign · A Renewable Kingdom

Sovereign Mechanism

Begin with raw resources

  1. Acquire one Kelp and tree seed, Water, a Lava source, initial Andesite and Iron. Establish farm and collection lanes.
  2. Water/Lava → Cobblestone → Gravel/Sand/Clay. Grow Kelp for Algal Blend and Dried Kelp. Wash Gravel for Iron.
  3. Craft one Makeshift Rotation Mechanism from 7 Andesite, 1 wooden slab and 1 Iron Ingot. Make two of these parts.
  4. Combine 2 Makeshift Rotation Mechanisms + 1 Andesite Casing → first Rotation Machine.
  5. Build the working power, Press, Basin, Mixer and Deployer using their complete construction recipes. Start Rotation assembly and expand the selector generators.

Mechanisms and infrastructure

Only the first three tiers use the Rotation, Hydraulic and Precision construction frames in this map. Later tiers identify their core infrastructure and consume the relevant mechanisms in the actual constructors. A finished apparatus or machine can exist before its tier's mechanism is mass-produced.

T&K3 · Production update

Raw resources to mechanisms

Earlier farms remain factory inputs throughout the campaign. Each path below follows the intended source recipe. Quantities in the workshop include the consumed ingredients for every pass.

Rotation · Roots of Industry

Grow Kelp and trees. Cobblestone supplies Gravel, Sand and Clay; gravel washing supplies Iron. An initial polished Andesite lens with a Rotation Machine generates Andesite without consuming the lens.

Ordered pass: Start with Wooden Slabs.

  1. Deploy Andesite Alloy Sheet.
  2. Deploy Cogwheel.
  3. Press the unfinished part.
  4. Deploy Wrench · retained.

2 pass(es) finish the mechanical output.

Open the exact mechanism recipe →

Sealed · Fields & Fluids

Seed Copper for the Hydraulic Veridium selector; keep the Copper Block lens. Smelt renewable Sand to Glass and craft Panes; press Iron to Sheets. Slime, Dried Kelp and Water make Rubber. Empty Tube and Spout construction currently form two starter cycles.

Ordered pass: Start with Rotation Mechanism.

  1. Deploy Copper Sheet.
  2. Deploy Empty Tube.
  3. Deploy Rubber.
  4. Fill 100 mB Water.
  5. Press the unfinished part.

2 pass(es) finish the mechanical output.

Open the exact mechanism recipe →

Precise · Into the Nether

Use Diorite for Quartz and Granite for Lapis. Precision selectors provide Scoria/Redstone, Scorchia/Coal, Asurine/Zinc and Ochrum/Gold. Copper + Zinc makes heated Brass. The consumed Netherrack and superheat supply for the new Lava route still need closure.

Ordered pass: Start with Sealed Mechanism.

  1. Deploy Brass Sheet.
  2. Deploy Electron Tube.
  3. Deploy Golden Sheet.
  4. Cut the unfinished part.
  5. Press the unfinished part.

2 pass(es) finish the mechanical output.

Open the exact mechanism recipe →

Calculation · The Connected Workshop

Multiply a Certus seed through water-grown buds and cluster harvesting. Make Rough Sand, Certus Dust, Siliceous Compound and Silicon. Retained native AE2 presses print circuits; retained Boot Medium completes processors. Engineering requires the new superheated Diamond path; Calculation also consumes Liquid XP.

Ordered pass: Start with Precision Mechanism.

  1. Deploy Logic Processor.
  2. Deploy Calculation Processor.
  3. Deploy Engineering Processor.
  4. Fill 100 mB Experience.
  5. Deploy Boot Medium · retained.
  6. Press the unfinished part.

1 pass(es) finish the mechanical output.

Open the exact mechanism recipe →

Inductive · Powered Industry

Grow Amethyst. Quartz and Redstone make Rose Quartz; Spectral Ruby supplies Singularities. Farm Ender Pearls, Gunpowder and dye materials; mix entanglement, crush dye singularities and mill the five paintball states into pigment. Chromatic Compound feeds Inductive assembly. After its initial ore seed, Steel Casing supports five Mekanism ore selectors.

Ordered pass: Start with Calculation Mechanism.

  1. Deploy Chromatic Compound.
  2. Deploy Basic Control Circuit.
  3. Deploy Copper Wire.
  4. Deploy Capacitor.
  5. Press the unfinished part.

2 pass(es) finish the mechanical output.

Open the exact mechanism recipe →

Arcane · The Living Workshop

Farm Ars Source, Source Gems and essences. Empty Tubes receive Polished Amethyst for Amethyst Tubes; Wizardry supplies Arcane Sheets. Water-grown Amethyst plus a retained Source Gem pedestal supports the new 2,500-Source Arcane Essence conversion. Finish the mechanical intermediate in the Apparatus.

Ordered pass: Start with Inductive Mechanism.

  1. Deploy Amethyst Tube.
  2. Deploy Arcane Sheet.
  3. Fill 100 mB Experience.
  4. Press the unfinished part.

2 pass(es) finish the mechanical output. Then use the Apparatus: central reagent Incomplete Abstruse Mechanism, pedestal inputs Source Gem, Manipulation Essence, Arcane Essence, Arcane Sheet, 5000 Source.

Chemical Engineering · Shadow Steel

Keep the renewable Chromatic/Shadow Steel chain running. Water electrolysis and the native Brine/Chlorine chain supply chemical production. Retain the Nether Star Focus in the assembly station; the first focus comes from a Wither progression acquisition.

  1. Deploy Shadow Steel Plate.
  2. Deploy Advanced Control Circuit.
  3. Fill 100 mB Experience.
  4. Deploy Sturdy Sheet.
  5. Deploy Nether Star Focus · retained.
  6. Press the unfinished part.

Containment · Nuclear Control

Gravel, Prismarine, Calcium Powder and Water compact into Suspicious Rock. Channeling gives probabilistic shell drops. Bone Blocks produce Dragonbone through Bulk Ending with a retained Dragon Head. Steel Casing geological Uranium/Fluorite feed nuclear processing. Upgrade and retain the Ignis Focus.

Ordered pass: Start with Chemical Engineering Mechanism.

  1. Deploy Overcharged Alloy Plate.
  2. Deploy Prismarine Alloy.
  3. Deploy Spiky Shell.
  4. Deploy Abjuration Essence.
  5. Deploy Elite Control Circuit.
  6. Deploy Ignis Focus · retained.
  7. Press the unfinished part.

2 pass(es) finish the mechanical output.

Singularity · Quantum Industry

Follow native reactor-waste and SPS processing to Antimatter. Amethyst feeds Echo Shard and Timeless Slurry; Empty Tubes become Blue Tubes. Renewable Stargaze/Radiant parts feed Singularity assembly. Blackstone plus Antimatter supplies Ancient Debris; XP fills Obsidian into Crying Obsidian. Retain Void Focus.

Ordered pass: Start with Containment Mechanism.

  1. Deploy Stargaze Alloy Plate.
  2. Deploy Radiant Coil.
  3. Deploy Blue Tube.
  4. Deploy Ultimate Control Circuit.
  5. Fill 250 mB Experience.
  6. Deploy Void Focus · retained.
  7. Press the unfinished part.

2 pass(es) finish the mechanical output.

Open the exact mechanism recipe →

Sovereign · A Renewable Kingdom

Join Charged Certus, Shadow Steel and XP with a retained Haunted Bell for Refined Quartz. Metal/XP lines make Tech Tubes; Radiant Obsidian, Atomic Alloy and AE2 Matter feed Circuit Scrap; the HDPE line feeds Matter Plastic. The three-pass final sequence retains Sovereign Focus. Every route inherits the unresolved earlier dependencies.

Ordered pass: Start with Singularity Mechanism.

  1. Deploy Matter Plastic.
  2. Deploy Tech Tube.
  3. Deploy Pellet Antimatter.
  4. Deploy Engineering Processor.
  5. Deploy Sovereign Focus · retained.
  6. Press the unfinished part.

3 pass(es) finish the mechanical output.

T&K3 · Production update

Wood, crop and animal feedstock

Acquire a sapling, then replant from drops and collect harvested Logs. Use the log/stripped-log and casing recipes in the catalogue. Maintain separate buffers for slabs, sticks, casing materials and replacement planting stock. Crop and animal farms supply Kelp, Wheat, Sugar, Eggs, dye materials and other recurring inputs.
