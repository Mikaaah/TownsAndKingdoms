Sovereign · A Renewable Kingdom

Sovereign Mechanism

Begin with raw resources

  1. Acquire one Kelp and tree seed, Water, a Lava source, initial Andesite and Iron. Establish farm and collection lanes.
  2. Water/Lava → Cobblestone → Gravel/Sand/Clay. Grow Kelp for Algal Blend and Dried Kelp. Wash Gravel for Iron.
  3. Craft one Makeshift Rotation Mechanism from 7 Andesite, 1 wooden slab and 1 Iron Ingot. Make two of these parts.
  4. Combine 2 Makeshift Rotation Mechanisms + 1 Andesite Casing → first Rotation Machine.
  5. Build the working power, Press, Basin, Mixer and Deployer using their complete construction recipes. Start Rotation assembly and expand the selector generators.

Mechanisms and infrastructure

Only the first three tiers use the Rotation, Hydraulic and Precision construction frames in this map. Later tiers identify their core infrastructure and consume the relevant mechanisms in the actual constructors. A finished apparatus or machine can exist before its tier's mechanism is mass-produced.

T&K3 · Repaired production

Raw resources to mechanisms

All ten main routes pass the static ingredient-and-station model. Quantities in the workshop include consumed materials for every assembly pass. World seeds and external stage unlocks remain explicit boundaries.

Rotation · Roots of Industry

Start with harvested Logs, Kelp, Clay, Andesite and Iron. Handcraft two Planks per Log or Wood block before powered Saw processing. Make the first Rotation Machine manually, then the Press, Basin and Deployer. Tree/crop farms and retained stone selectors feed ongoing production.

Start with: Wooden Slabs.

  1. Deploy Andesite Alloy Sheet.
  2. Deploy Cogwheel.
  3. Press the unfinished part.
  4. Deploy Wrench · retained.

2 assembly passes.

Sealed · Fields & Fluids

Smelt the first Raw Copper, press Copper Sheets and compact two Sheets plus one Copper Ingot into four Fluid Pipes. The Spout and Pump use a Rotation Machine. Compact six Glass Panes plus two Iron Sheets into four Empty Tubes before a Mechanical Crafter is available. Rubber, tubes, copper and pumped Water feed Sealed assembly.

Start with: Rotation Mechanism.

  1. Deploy Copper Sheet.
  2. Deploy Empty Tube.
  3. Deploy Rubber.
  4. Fill with 100 mB Water.
  5. Press the unfinished part.

2 assembly passes.

Precise · Into the Nether

Produce Brass and Nether materials for Precision assembly. Retained selectors provide stone and mineral feedstock. Separate Nether Bricks/Precision Machine and Polished Blackstone/Precision Machine selectors now generate Netherrack and Blackstone. Netherrack feeds the native Cinder Flour and Blaze Cake chain.

Start with: Sealed Mechanism.

  1. Deploy Brass Sheet.
  2. Deploy Electron Tube.
  3. Deploy Golden Sheet.
  4. Cut the unfinished part.
  5. Press the unfinished part.

Calculation · The Connected Workshop

Obtain the first Certus seed and four AE2 meteorite presses. Grow Certus, charge crystals, print circuits and use retained presses during processor assembly. Calculation also supplies the repaired early Ars and XP constructors. Verify first FE, Source and XP collection in game.

  2. Deploy Calculation Processor.
  3. Deploy Engineering Processor.
  4. Fill with 100 mB Experience.
  5. Deploy Boot Medium · retained.
  6. Press the unfinished part.

Inductive · Powered Industry

Grow Amethyst from a seed; produce Rose Quartz, entangled materials, five paintball states, pigment and Chromatic Compound. The first Basic Control Circuit is bootstrapped through Create. First End/Dragon access and mineral seeds remain world boundaries. Milling implements the current pigment/paintball progression.

Start with: Calculation Mechanism.

  1. Deploy Chromatic Compound.
  2. Deploy Basic Control Circuit.
  3. Deploy Copper Wire.
  4. Deploy Capacitor.
  5. Press the unfinished part.

Arcane · The Living Workshop

Farm Source and repeatable Source Gems/essences. Make the unfinished Arcane assembly and finish it in the Enchanting Apparatus. Early PRC, Separator and Rotary constructors use Inductive; the Chemical Infuser and Osmium Compressor use Arcane. These stations precede late antimatter production.

T&K3 · Production update

Wood, crop and animal feedstock

Acquire a sapling, then replant from drops and collect harvested Logs. Handcraft two Planks per Log or Wood block to start; the powered Saw keeps its better yield. Use the log/stripped-log and casing recipes in the catalogue. Maintain separate buffers for slabs, sticks, casing materials and replacement planting stock. Crop and animal farms supply Kelp, Wheat, Sugar, Eggs, dye materials and other recurring inputs.
