# Chapter 02 · Andesite

Affordable repeated machinery and the first automated material loop.

## Main production route

**Wooden Slab → apply Andesite Alloy → apply Andesite Alloy → Iron Hammer → Rotation Mechanism.**

The hammer takes durability. Eight Rotation Mechanisms around an Andesite Casing craft the same machine as the manual route. Mixer Alloy costs one Andesite and one Clay Ball.

## Your milestone

Produce **Rotation Mechanism** consistently before expanding its consumers. Keep a buffer of the previous chapter’s components and a route for incomplete items to continue through the line.
