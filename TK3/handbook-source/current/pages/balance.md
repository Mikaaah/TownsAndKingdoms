# Balance & removed shortcuts

The current pack changes individual crafting/acquisition routes and selected items. It does not impose an automatic chapter lock on every dimension.

## Factory direction

Create replaces routine IE manufacturing and AE2 Inscriber processing. IE's distinctive heavy machines and AE2's storage/autocrafting remain useful.

Growth Accelerators, Crystal Fixer and the ExtendedAE Circuit Cutter are disabled. Natural budding and in-world crystal mechanics remain available.

## Selected acquisition restrictions

The shared catalogue disables Ender Leads, selected mob-capture upgrades, the Spatial Anchor, IE Chunk Loader, the craftable Alex's Caves Nuclear Bomb, selected novelty/flight items and duplicate Rubberworks rubber materials.

Primed Alex's Caves nuclear bomb entities are also blocked by the custom hook. Toxic Caves, the Nuclear Furnace and other nuclear systems remain.

Capturing enchantment is removed from the configured acquisition tags and denied to the printer. Bosses, including the Wither and Dragon, are protected from Ars jar capture.

## Spawners and flight

The spawner runes that ignore conditions, ignore players or remove AI are blocked by the cleanup rules. The ordinary spawner system and single-use Workshop Spawner Cage are separate features.

The selected Apotheosis flying potion route/effect is disabled. The **Angel Ring** is the planned late-game flight item, with an **11 × 7 Mechanical Crafter** wing recipe at the Arcane tier.

Its forty consumed ingredients include feathers, Gold Blocks, Legendary Ink, Arcane materials, Ignitium, Air Essence, Nether Stars, Wind Charges, a Diamond Ring, Cloud in a Bottle and Golden Amber. Native Ring flight behavior is not rewritten by the recipe.

## Food and recycling

The three confirmed infinite-food items are disabled conditionally when registered. Ordinary meals remain.

The nine-metal ore chain blocks finished metal from returning to its multiplying Dust/Crushed stages. Native Netherite Scrap yields are not increased.

## Visibility is not deletion

Legacy decorative/custom entries and creative-only items can be hidden without being unregistered. Existing worlds may retain stacks and placed blocks. The exact shared catalogue is included in the [source reference](../status/).

Configuration and beta testing can still change the final release. This guide records the inspected package's selected rules rather than claiming every unrelated mod interaction has been tested.
