# Chapter 04 · Brass

Heated alloys, precise assembly, controls and Crushing Wheels.

## Main production route

**Sealed Mechanism → Golden Sheet → Brass Sheet → Electron Tube → Precision Mechanism.**

One pass produces one guaranteed mechanism. Eight Precision Mechanisms around a Brass Casing make a Brass Machine. Normal heated Brass mixing and Quartz polishing remain. Crushing Wheels begin the 2× raw-ore route.

## Your milestone

Produce **Precision Mechanism** consistently before expanding its consumers. Keep a buffer of the previous chapter’s components and a route for incomplete items to continue through the line.
