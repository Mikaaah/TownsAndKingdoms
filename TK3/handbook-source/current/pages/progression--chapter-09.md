# Chapter 09 · Eclipse

Chromatic materials, ink recovery and the 5× ore finish.

## Main production route

**Arcane Mechanism → Radiant Coil → Radiant Coil → Refined Shadow Alloy → Spectral Ruby Tube → fill 200 mB Uncommon Ink → Press → Eclipse Mechanism.**

The substrate is the Arcane Mechanism. The Eclipse Lens turns four matching metal Dust into five Ingots. Use native Create Chromatic Compound, Refined Radiance and Shadow Steel. Stop the paintball drain chain at Purple for Chromatic production.

## Your milestone

Produce **Eclipse Mechanism** consistently before expanding its consumers. Keep a buffer of the previous chapter’s components and a route for incomplete items to continue through the line.
