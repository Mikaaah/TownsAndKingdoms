# Magic that meets the workshop

Ars Nouveau supplies spell design, Source and magical automation. Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks supplies combat schools, scrolls, spellbooks and equipment. Their integrations let both systems contribute to the factory.

## Ars Nouveau

Develop spell components through Ars' own systems. Source Gems, Sourceberries and compatible focuses connect to Arcane production. Existing Source costs and glyph requirements remain on focus recipes; optional exploration ingredients add alternatives.

Ars Creo and Create Wizardry connect mechanical and magical work. A Mana Siphon exists, but the current Mana bootstrap does not require one: **Arcane Essence plus Water** makes Mana through the defined Mixer route.

## Iron's Spells and Resonant Arcana

Choose equipment and schools for the way you fight. The native Scroll Forge and research acquisition remain relevant.

**Resonant Arcana** is the custom spell addon. Its individual spell roster and balance change with the installed build. The wiki does not turn older proposed spells or hotfix test logs into a fixed current spell-count claim.

Ars 'n Spells provides the selected unified-mana integration. Origins and skill bonuses should apply once through their own supported attributes.

## Build a two-Deployer scroll line

The script discovers enabled, Forge-craftable spells and valid native ink outcomes, including eligible addon spells in supported schools.

1. Put **Paper** under the first Deployer and supply a valid **school focus**.
2. Install an allow-list **Create Filter** containing the desired Unfinished School Scroll **and that held focus**. Enable **Respect Data**.
3. Feed the unfinished scroll to the second Deployer and supply the matching **Ink**.
4. Its Filter must contain **one exact completed spell/level scroll and that Ink**, also with Respect Data.

Each completed scroll consumes one Paper, one focus and one Ink. Filters remain reusable. Including the held ingredient allows the Deployer to restock its hand.

No filter, the wrong school/ink, missing component data or multiple selectable outputs stop processing rather than choosing an arbitrary spell. A direct scroll ghost is not a substitute for the configured list Filter.

Research-required and noncraftable spells retain their native acquisition. Ink cannot be filtered into a stronger outcome than its native rarity permits.

The current startup/rarity correction removes the former automatic whole-pack reload. Powered full-pack operation remains a beta test item.

## Witchery and other magical paths

Just Another Witchery Remake supplies its own ritual/discovery path. It is a selected separate quest direction. This wiki does not restore old Malum or Botania recipes merely because inherited quest text mentions them.

## Alchemical Lasers

The five lenses are **Growth, Convergence, Splitting, Power and Eclipse**. Their roles include crystal growth, material conversion and late ore processing. Follow the lens and minimum speed in each recipe.

The current Calculation chapter requires a **Growth Lens**; the old 0.1.0 JAR embedded in the base export is insufficient. Use the matching newer pack build. Lenses are tooling, while recipe input counts still apply.
