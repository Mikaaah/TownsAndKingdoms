# Credits & community

**Towns & Kingdoms 3 | Beyond the Forge** is developed by Mikaaah with its community.

The pack builds on the work of the Create, NeoForge, KubeJS, FTB, AE2, Immersive Engineering, Ars Nouveau, Iron's Spells, Epic Fight and many other mod teams. Every entry in the [mod inventory](../mods/) links to its project and names its author.

Custom development includes Towns & Kingdoms Workshop, Towns & Kingdoms Progression, Unidentified Loot, Resonant Arcana, Alchemical Lasers, Flow Control and the Wildshape terrain direction.

Branding and supplied pixel artwork retain their creators' rights. In-game feature assets are not offered here as standalone art packs.

The original TK2 wiki is preserved in the [archive](../2.0/), including historical version numbers and unfinished pages. Its content describes the older pack.
