# Chapter 01 · Manual

Clay, Andesite Alloy and the first manually crafted machine body.

## Main production route

**Four Alloy + four wooden slabs + one Iron Ingot make a Makeshift Mechanism. Eight around an Andesite Casing make the first Andesite Machine.**

Use ordinary wood crafting and Create casing application. Makeshift construction is restricted to player crafting menus.

## Your milestone

Produce **Makeshift Mechanism** consistently before expanding its consumers. Keep a buffer of the previous chapter’s components and a route for incomplete items to continue through the line.
