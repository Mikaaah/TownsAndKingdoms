# The Beta 1.0 questbook

Use the book to find your next project, learn a system and track your progress. Beta 1.0 includes the rebuilt chapter layout and the first populated production chapters, alongside inherited quest material.

**The book remains in progress.** The target is at least **1,500 useful quests**. The archive's raw record count includes legacy chapters and unfinished pages, so it does not measure completion of the new campaign.

## The new groups

| Group | What belongs here |
|---|---|
| Town Square | Tutorial, progression overview, Market and Bulletin Board |
| Forging Ahead | The ten main production chapters |
| Crafts & Curiosities | Aeronautics, magic, fishing, nuclear engineering, enchanting and Let's Do paths |
| Realms & Relics | Exploration, Bosses, Useful Items and The Eyes |
| Seasonal Tales | The First Winter seasonal page |

Some of these pages are placeholders while their objectives and rewards are authored. Older Town Square, Mechanical, Adventure and Magical groups also remain in the exported book.

## Town Square and specialist paths

The Market and Bulletin Board provide the shopping and job-board direction. Their new pages and final reward assignments are still being built.

Specialist pages cover Aeronautics, Witchery, Starcatcher, Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, Ars Nouveau, Create Nuclear, Enchantment Industry and Let's Do. Exploration brings together Alex's Caves, Twilight Forest, Aether and Deeper and Darker; Bosses and The Eyes track encounter and End Remastered goals.

The First Winter page establishes a Seasonal Tales branch. Its presence does not mean the seasonal questline is complete.

## Ponder from a quest

Hover a supported item in a task and hold **W**. The [97-lesson reference](../ponder/) includes stone generation, processors, mechanisms, industrial multiblocks and Workshop features. The beta includes custom quest icons and artwork with its KubeJS resources.

## Reading inherited chapters
