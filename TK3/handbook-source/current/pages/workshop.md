# Towns & Kingdoms Workshop

Workshop contains the pack's custom utility features. It gives your settlement tools for displaying goals, building, handling selected entities and connecting player destinations.

## Quest Projector

Place the Projector and right-click to configure its display. Supported modes include **quests with tasks, images and formatted text**. Saved display settings travel with the configured item.

Use it for a workshop objective board, a town sign or a visible shared project. Quest displays read the supported quest integration; an image or text projection does not create a new reward or complete a task.

## Construction tools

The supplied JAR includes **Measuring Pencil**, **Builder's Satchel** and **Builder's Brush**. Their in-game controls and tooltips describe the selected operation. They are practical building utilities, rather than substitute machine frames.

**Magic Mirror**, **Chorus Mirror** and **Mob Cage** are also present. Dimension, destination and containment restrictions can apply. Boss protection remains relevant; the pack blocks boss jar capture, including the Dragon and Wither.

## Spawner Cage

Right-click a spawner to capture it, then right-click a block to place the captured spawner. The Cage is **consumed after placement**. It is a single-use relocation tool, not a reusable spawner farm multiplier.

Spawner tooltips expose useful settings such as spawn count, delay range, activation range, spawn range and nearby mob limit. The reward/acquisition route is a separate pack decision; no free recipe is invented here.

## Trading Hub

[Open the Trading Hub guide](../trading-hub/) for placement, listing, browsing and teleportation. It uses Waystones/Balm and retains the stable item ID <code>tnkcore:trade_sharestone</code>.

## Ponder compatibility

Workshop 0.11.10 includes the optional client integrations for IE multiblock rendering in Ponder and Enchantment Industry bulk-salvaging with Apotheosis materials.

The separate older Ponder-render helper is superseded by that build. Match Workshop on clients and the server; keep the full corresponding Ponder resources.

## Discord presence

Workshop includes configurable Rich Presence for supported activities, locations and menus. Availability depends on its configuration and a supported desktop Discord session. Presence is a cosmetic integration, not a gameplay requirement.
