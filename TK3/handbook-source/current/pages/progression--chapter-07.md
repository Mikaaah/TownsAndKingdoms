# Chapter 07 · Industrial

IE heavy processing and the 4× ore chain.

## Main production route

**Reinforced Mechanism → Steel Plate → Advanced Electronic Component → Steel Mechanical Component → Screwdriver → Press → Industrial Mechanism.**

The screwdriver takes one durability per assembly. Advanced Electronic Components use Rubber, two IE Electron Tubes, Aluminium Wire and a Press. Haunt Obsidian into Crying Obsidian, then use the IE Crusher for Aluminium and Nickel dust.

## Your milestone

Produce **Industrial Mechanism** consistently before expanding its consumers. Keep a buffer of the previous chapter’s components and a route for incomplete items to continue through the line.
