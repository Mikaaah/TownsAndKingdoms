# A factory that keeps working

Make reliable inputs before increasing speed. A slow line with a buffer is easier to expand than a fast line that starves every other machine.

## Chute stone generators

These generators use ordinary Water/Lava formation and Create's Mechanical Drill optimization. The **normal Chute goes directly below the generated block**. The **catalyst goes directly below the adjacent Lava source**. The two positions are different.

Keep the Water source separate from the output and use a drop trench for flowing Water where the Ponder shows it. Place the Drill behind the formation cell, facing it. Establish fluids and supports before starting the machine.

| Catalyst under Lava | Generated block |
|---|---|
| Andesite | Andesite |
| Veridium | Veridium |
| Ochrum | Ochrum |
| Asurine | Asurine |
| Scoria | Scoria |
| Diorite | Diorite |
| Crimsite | Crimsite |
| Limestone | Limestone |

Catalysts are retained. A Smart Chute, Hopper or catalyst below the output does not activate the custom rule. Without the matching arrangement, normal formation remains. **Replace the Drill after changing the catalyst** so Create refreshes its cached prediction.

Hover the stone item in a quest task and hold **W** for the in-game construction lesson.

## Ore yields increase with your factory

| Stage | Operation | Yield per original Raw |
|---|---|---:|
| Manual / Andesite | Smelting or Millstone processing | 1× |
| Brass | Crushing Wheels: 1 Raw → 2 Crushed | 2× |
| Industrial | IE Crusher: each Crushed → 2 Dust | 4× |
| Eclipse | Eclipse Lens: 4 Dust → 5 Ingots | 5× |

The shared chain covers **Iron, Copper, Gold, Zinc, Aluminium, Nickel, Lead, Silver and Uranium**. Zinc has a real custom dust. Packed Raw blocks yield 18 Crushed. Refined metal cannot be recycled back into a multiplying stage.

Raw-ore doubling does not add three metal nuggets. Renewable ore-stone recipes can retain their specified nugget bonus. Crushing Wheels receive one 10% Experience Nugget roll, with the exactly-one-paintball recipe excluded. Millstone normalization retains primary-output rolls and removes unrelated byproducts from recipes it can safely decode.

## Keep assemblies aligned

Use adjacent Deployers for successive operations; empty spacing is unnecessary. Put an unpowered or stopped buffer before your line, feed each hand the correct ingredient, and arrange a return route only when the recipe needs repeated passes.

**Reusable** does not always mean **free of wear**. Iron Hammers, Certus Wrenches and Screwdrivers lose durability. Processor dies and the Creation assembly’s Boot Drive are retained without wear.

## Reduce dropped-item traffic

Collect stone directly through Chutes, use storage buffers at handoff points, and upgrade to the supported [Infinity Cells](../storage/) when their material tier is available. Those cells replace repeated stone production inside ME storage; they do not duplicate arbitrary items.
