# Chapter 08 · Arcane

Source, Mana and magical materials join the production line.

## Main production route

**Industrial Mechanism → fill 400 mB Dragon’s Breath → Source Gem → Sturdy Sheet → Mana Tube → Arcane Mechanism.**

There is no finishing Press or Tuning Fork in this sequence. A Mana Tube holds 300 mB Mana. Superheat Lapis, Amethyst and an Experience Nugget into Arcane Essence; mix one Essence with 250 mB Water for 250 mB Mana. The Angel Ring uses four Arcane Mechanisms in its large mechanical craft.

## Your milestone

Produce **Arcane Mechanism** consistently before expanding its consumers. Keep a buffer of the previous chapter’s components and a route for incomplete items to continue through the line.
