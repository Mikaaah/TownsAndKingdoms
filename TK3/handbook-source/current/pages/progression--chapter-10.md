# Chapter 10 · Creation

The final shared project for your entire factory.

## Main production route

**Unstable Creation Core → apply Rotation, Sealed, Precision, Calculation, Reinforced, Industrial, Arcane, Eclipse and Matter Construct → repeat that nine-step pass → Creation Core.**

Eighteen ingredient deployments consume two of each listed mechanism and two Matter Constructs. Each Matter Construct and the Unstable Core need an Unstable Creation Gem: at least three gems per Core, excluding any other upstream costs. The Boot Drive is retained without durability loss in this assembly.

## Your milestone

Produce **Creation Core** consistently before expanding its consumers. Keep a buffer of the previous chapter’s components and a route for incomplete items to continue through the line.

## Supporting lines

Use the Arc Furnace for Lapis Alloy, charging for Overcharge Alloy, and repeated pressing for the advanced sheets. An Eclipse Lens processes a Dye Entangled Singularity into an Unstable Creation Gem over **1,000 ticks at a minimum 32 RPM**. The Boot Drive is made from a filled 16k Item Cell, Radiant Coil and Lapis Block.

The Unstable Core sequence consumes **500 mB Lava and 500 mB Mana**, plus its sheets, while retaining the Boot Drive without wear. The final Creation Machine craft consumes eight Creation Cores and a Netherite Block. It currently has no assigned processing, power or infinite-resource function.
