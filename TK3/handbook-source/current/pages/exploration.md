# Explore, fight, return

Build a useful home, then take a reason to leave it. Dungeons, caves, dimensions and bosses bring back equipment, building palettes and ingredients with practical uses.

## The world beyond your base

The selected environment combines Regions Unexplored, Biomes O' Plenty, Nature's Spirit and terrain/river integrations. **Wildshape** is the project's terrain direction; the supplied base export still carries UltraTerraForged-era JAR names and presets.

Streams Reflowing adds flowing rivers, with **Flow Control** supplying river-editing tools. Terrain generation and pregeneration affect newly generated chunks. The wiki does not promise that a later preset has already changed an existing server world.

The supplied export's biome toggles do not yet match every later curation decision. A final published preset and inventory remain on the release checklist.

The pack keeps dimension access outside blanket workshop stage locks. Native portal costs, End Remastered and Twilight's own rules still apply.

## Dungeons and encounters

YUNG's structures, Integrated structures, Cataclysm, Mowzie's and other selected encounter mods provide different scales of adventure. Structure integrations aim to place compatible encounters coherently; universal spawn or structure parity is not claimed.

Lootr supports per-player container loot where compatible. **Unidentified Loot** connects equipment discoveries to identification, rarity, collection and salvage.

## Combat preparation

Epic Fight, Weapons of Miracles and Nightfall form the selected action-combat family. Carry a weapon suited to its moveset and check your keybinds after updating addons.

Punchy-related compatibility is being developed alongside the pack. The supplied base export does not independently establish every later Combat Punchy build or resolved rendering issue. The current wiki does not claim the armor/face compatibility reports are all fixed.

## Materials worth bringing home

Alex's Caves resources have optional routes into magnets, AE2 parts, glue, fertilizer, Arcane Essence and Glow Ink. Aether and Twilight materials can also appear in their own Excavator deposit pools.

These alternate uses complement ordinary discovery. They do not replace every native crafting path or force a boss catalyst into every machine.

## The Eyes and boss rematches

The custom End Remastered mapping file has no added boss/chest assignments. Native eye sources remain. The proposed repeatable boss bounty/token flow belongs to the questbook rebuild; its final supported boss list and costs need the matching configured export.
