# Weapon catalogue artwork

The catalogue includes 602 item images. Sprites and inventory model previews come from the supplied TNK3 pack resources and their installed Minecraft 1.21.1 mod releases. Pack resource overrides take precedence. The artwork remains the work of its respective authors.

Flat items use their native texture layers. Model previews use native geometry, UVs and inventory orientation, shown in a static base pose or first animation frame. Unsupported runtime renderers and unresolved native model references retain category symbols; those symbols do not represent the item model.

The item-art import uses the supplied CB-1.1.3 resources. Combat assignments and numbers retain the catalogue’s stated CB-1.1.2 reference.

Exact archive hashes, item resource paths, export methods and unavailable models are recorded in [icon-sources.json](icon-sources.json). No mod JARs are published.
