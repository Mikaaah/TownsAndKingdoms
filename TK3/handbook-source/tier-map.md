# MODS, ADDONS & THEIR TIERS

**ONE CAMPAIGN · TEN WORKSHOPS · CONNECTED SYSTEMS.**

This map follows the current selected mod list and every addon / support entry. A tier means access to the relevant workshop or encounter branch; terrain, UI mods and required libraries keep native roles. Native rituals, boss seals, research, channels, power consumption and flight physics still matter.

## The ten-tier route

| Chapter | Workshop | Main systems |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Roots of Industry | Algal binder, rotation and your first workshop |
| **2** | Copperworks | Sealed assembly, tanks and fluid handling |
