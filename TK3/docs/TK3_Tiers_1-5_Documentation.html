Your first steps

  1. Craft Algal Blend from kelp and clay.
  2. Combine the blend with andesite to make Andesite Alloy.
  3. Craft a Kinetic Machine from seven Andesite Alloy, one wooden slab and one Andesite Casing (AAA / ACA / ASA).
  4. Use a stonecutter to turn the frame into workshop machines.
  5. Connect power, then add a press, basin, mixer and deployer to improve your material efficiency.
Follow the chapter quests to unlock the next machine tier. Item tasks check what you own; setup checkmarks are your confirmation that the workshop works.
02 / Build your machines

One frame, a choice of machines

Choose the machine you need in the stonecutter.

Machine frames organise the workshop into clear tiers. The first frame has an expensive manual recipe and a cheaper automated recipe. Each automated frame uses its casing and one deployed mechanism. Mechanisms carry the previous tier into the new assembly line.

Tier Frame How it is made Role
1 Kinetic Machine Andesite Casing → deploy one Kinetic Mechanism; manual startup: 7 Andesite Alloy + wooden slab + casing Basic workshop machines
2 Hydraulic Machine Copper Casing → deploy one Sealed Mechanism Fluid devices
3 Precision Machine Brass Casing → deploy one Precision Mechanism Smart machinery and logistics
4 Arcane Machine Wizardry Arcane Casing → deploy one Arcane Mechanism Apparatus-based magical devices
5 Steel Casing 5 steel + 2 osmium + Precision Machine + Arcane Machine Mekanism and first FE
Stonecutting consumes the frame. One Kinetic Machine can become three water wheels, two basins or one press, mixer or deployer. These are separate choices, not a combined bundle.

Mechanism sequences always have one loop and a guaranteed result. The last step uses a deployer-held tool, with no extra press afterwards. Arcane devices use apparatus recipes instead of stonecutting. Steel Casings connect the mechanical and magical workshops to FE.

03 / Follow the chain

Tier walkthroughs

What to gather, which process to use and how to reach the next workshop.

Follow these routes to build each workshop. Use the recipe catalogue for exact ingredients and patterns. Native means an existing recipe or mechanic from Minecraft or one of the mods.

First workshop: native crafting table, furnace and stonecutter → manual blend and alloy → stripped log + alloy casing → raw-material frame → water wheel, shafts and cogs → press and basin → mixer and deployer → cheaper automated mechanisms and frames.

A vanilla stonecutter needs iron and stone, without a custom machine prerequisite. Mine the first iron or wash gravel. Press iron into sheets; craft the Wrench, Whisk and windmill parts using the explicit Create support recipes. The manual casing uses a stripped log and one Andesite Alloy; the automated casing uses the same materials in a Deployer. One frame can become three water wheels, two basins or one press, mixer or deployer. These are separate choices: stonecutting consumes the frame.

Saw processing follows log → stripped log → six planks. If the mod has no matching stripped variant, the saw produces planks directly. Bamboo blocks yield three planks. Native handcrafting remains the starting route.

Tier 2 · Sealed & Hydraulic

Sealant: 1 kelp + 1 wheat + 250 mB water → mixer → 2 slimeballs. Fill the first basin using a bucket; the recipe does not require a pump. Slime obtained from mobs is also an approved material source.

Sealed Mechanism: Kinetic Mechanism → deploy copper sheet → deploy slimeball → finish with a deployer holding Farmer’s Delight Iron Knife. Three operations, one loop, guaranteed output.

Hydraulic Machine: Copper Casing → deploy one Sealed Mechanism → Hydraulic Machine. Stonecut one frame into sixteen fluid pipes, three tanks, or a pump, spout, drain, hose pulley or another listed fluid device.

Tier 3 · Brass & Precision

Brass: an Empty Blaze Burner holding a captured blaze provides heat. Copper ingot + mined zinc ingot → heated mixer → 2 brass ingots. Press brass into sheets. Craft or mix Rose Quartz, polish it with Sand Paper (by hand or with a Deployer), then use a Press-produced Iron Sheet to make Electron Tubes.

Precision Mechanism: Sealed Mechanism → deploy brass sheet → deploy Electron Tube → finish with a deployer holding Create Sand Paper. Three operations, one loop.

Precision Machine: Brass Casing → deploy one Precision Mechanism → Precision Machine. Stonecut the frame into mechanical crafters, arms, controllers and smart logistics devices.

Crushing Wheels: 16 Andesite Alloy + 4 planks + 1 Precision Machine → mechanical crafting → 2 wheels. Build the crafters from a frame first; wheels are not required to make their own crafting machines. Milling starts earlier, while crushing improves geological yields from tier 3.

Tier 4 · Arcane Industry

Early materials: native Imbuement Chamber → Source Gems and Ars essences. Source Gem → soul-fire fan haunting → Iron’s Arcane Essence. Native Source Jars and Volcanic/Vitalic Sourcelinks supply the first Source. These sourcelinks become available after chapter 3 and retain native recipes.

Enchanting Apparatus: Precision Machine + diamond + Source Gem → crafting. This makes the apparatus reachable before Arcane Machines.

Arcane Mechanism: Precision Mechanism → deploy Source Gem → deploy Arcane Essence → deploy Manipulation Essence → deploy gold ingot → finish with a deployer holding Ars Enchanter’s Sword. Five operations, one loop, one guaranteed mechanism.

Arcane Casing: Brass Casing as reagent; Source Gem, Arcane Essence and gold on pedestals → apparatus, 500 Source → Wizardry Arcane Casing.

Arcane Machine: Wizardry Arcane Casing → deploy one Arcane Mechanism → Arcane Machine. Use it for the selected Source devices, automation charms and Wizardry machines. Native first-Source generation avoids a circular dependency.

Runes and sheets: stone + Arcane Essence → compacting → Blank Rune. Blank Rune as reagent, with the relevant Ars essence and an Arcane Mechanism → apparatus → fire, ice, lightning or nature rune. Iron ingot as reagent, with Source Gem, Arcane Essence and gold → apparatus → Arcane Ingot → pressing → Wizardry Arcane Sheet.

Ink, route A: ink sac + Arcane Essence + 250 mB water → mixer → 2 Common Ink items.

Ink, route B: 250 mB Wizardry Mana + ink sac → alchemist cauldron → 250 mB Common Ink fluid. A glass bottle empties 250 mB into one Common Ink item.

Higher inks, glyphs and combat progression retain their native routes. Complete the Wilden challenge to obtain the permanent industrial catalyst.

Tier 5 · Steel & FE

Steel bootstrap: 2 iron ingots + 1 coal → heated mixer → 2 steel ingots.

Steel Casing: 5 steel ingots + 2 osmium ingots + 1 Precision Machine + 1 Arcane Machine → shaped crafting. Find osmium in the world and use native smelting for the first ingots.

First power: casing → Heat Generator and basic cables → FE. No FE is needed to construct the first generator.

Infuser: Steel Casing + Wilden Tribute → deployer → Metallurgic Infuser. The held Tribute is retained. Iron with native redstone infusion produces Infused Alloy, opening the Enrichment Chamber.

Refining: raw iron, copper, gold, osmium, tin or lead → enrichment → 2 dust → native smelting → 2 ingots. Native ingot-to-dust conversion and later ore chemistry remain available.

Second steel route: native infuser processing → steel dust → the explicit Mekanism smelting recipe → steel ingot. Mixer steel and infuser steel are both deliberately approved.

Alternator and Electric Motor connect rotation with FE. Native spool and rolling-mill support remains. Sophisticated diamond containers and Stack Upgrade 2 become available here. Container progression is copper → iron → gold → diamond; skip-tier routes are removed. Netherite, omega, infinity and higher stack tiers are reserved for later.

Finishing tools and chapter rewards

Each mechanism sequence ends with a tool held by a Deployer. The tool is not a belt ingredient. Normal tools lose one durability per finishing operation. A chapter’s permanent reward is the same item with the Unbreakable component, so it fits the same recipe and stays in the deployer.

Chapter Final tool Milestone reward
I BetterEnd Iron Hammer Unbreakable Workshop Hammer
II Farmer’s Delight Iron Knife Unbreakable Sealwright Knife
III Create Sand Paper Unbreakable Precision Abrasive
IV Ars Enchanter’s Sword Unbreakable Arcane Engraver
V Earlier workshop tools A second unbreakable Iron Hammer for a parallel kinetic line

Craft and use an ordinary tool before finishing the chapter; the reward removes maintenance afterwards. Every player may claim each milestone reward once. It is not a recipe ingredient or an unlock token. On a server, a team can supply multiple finishing deployers by claiming its members’ rewards.

The Iron Hammer’s native shaped recipe uses four iron ingots and two sticks. Farmer’s Delight Iron Knife retains its native recipe. Create Sand Paper uses the explicit paper + sand support recipe. The Enchanter’s Sword uses its native Enchanting Apparatus recipe: diamond sword as reagent; one diamond, two gold blocks and two Source Gem Blocks on pedestals; no Source cost. Your apparatus and first Source generation are available before the Arcane Machine.

Put the starting item on a belt or depot and perform each deployment in order. Two alloys means two separate deployments, even when the same deployer is reused. All four sequences have one loop and a guaranteed output. A finishing tool replaces the old final press; there is no extra pressing operation afterwards.

All mechanisms require sequenced assembly. Only the tier 1 frame has a manual startup recipe from raw materials; automated frames use a casing plus one deployed mechanism. Stonecutting then selects the actual machine.

Create support recipes

The Create parts guide groups casings, sheets, tools, windmill parts, storage and railway components by tier. Every entry opens its exact recipe.

Tier 1: startup casings, iron/gold sheets, shafts, windmill parts, filters and tools.

Tier 2: copper casings/sheets, Item Vaults, diving equipment, nozzles and the Empty Blaze Burner. Capture a blaze using the burner before the heated brass route.

Tier 3: brass casings/sheets, Rose Quartz, Electron Tubes, advanced filters, trains and factory logistics. Material packing is reversible; clear recipes reset configured filters and schedules.

05 / Keep materials flowing

Resource generators

Each stone has its own renewable material route.

Build the foundation

Start with a normal lava/water cobblestone or stone generator. Under the position where the new stone appears, place the lens block from the table. Place the required frame one block below the lens.

Milling is available early. Crushing gives better yields once you can build Crushing Wheels in tier 3. Nine washed nuggets equal one ingot.

Timber processing

Use the saw for log → stripped log → six planks. Woods without a matching stripped variant produce planks directly. Bamboo blocks give three planks. Handcrafting remains available for startup.

Storage that grows with the workshop

Progress through copper → iron → gold → diamond containers. Upgrade recipes carry the contents and settings forward. Basic utilities start early; advanced utilities and Stack Upgrade 1 follow precision; diamond containers and Stack Upgrade 2 arrive with industry.

07 / Useful answers

Workshop tips

Small details that make the production chain easier.

How do I automate slimeballs?

Mix one kelp, one wheat and 250 mB water to make two slimeballs. Farm the two ingredients and feed them into a basin. Use a bucket for the first water supply, then connect a pump when the hydraulic workshop is ready.

Do I need a pump before making a Hydraulic Machine?

No. Fill the first basin with a bucket. The sealant route then makes Sealed Mechanisms, which let you assemble the Hydraulic Machine and cut it into a pump.

How do I get Source before building arcane machines?

Use a Source Jar with a native Volcanic or Vitalic Sourcelink after chapter 3. Make Source Gems and essences with the Imbuement Chamber. This supplies the first apparatus recipes before you build the Agronomic Sourcelink.

Is the Wilden Tribute used up?

No. The deployer keeps the held Tribute while converting a Steel Casing into an Infuser. Keep this permanent catalyst for further machines.

How do I make the first steel without FE?

Use a heated mixer: two iron ingots and one coal make two steel ingots. Build a casing, Heat Generator and cables before the powered Infuser route.

Why are later machines unavailable?

Complete the previous chapter’s milestone to unlock the next machine tier. AE2 and higher industrial layers follow in later chapters.
