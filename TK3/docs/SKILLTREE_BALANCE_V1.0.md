# T&K3 Skill Tree — v1.0 Balance Baseline

## Tuning order

1. Archetype root identity
2. Class-core rank chains
3. Subclass root and keystone identity
4. Apothic attribute budgets
5. Iron's school power totals
6. Profession loot/resource effects
7. Maximum skill-point cap

Do not balance an overtuned player tree by immediately inflating enemy health.

## Apothic budget philosophy

Keep additive percentage-point stats low.

- crit chance: small single-digit percentage-point increases
- dodge: small single-digit percentage-point increases
- armor shred: low percentages
- armor pierce: small flat values
- life steal: very small percentages
- projectile/draw-speed bonuses: low percentage increases
- XP/mining bonuses: utility-scale, not exponential progression

The objective is for Apotheosis gear to complement a build, not replace the skill tree or class choice.

## Mastery limits

- two profession masteries
- two universal combat keystones
- two Iron's school masteries

These limits preserve identity even if the final point cap becomes generous.

## Initial point target

Start balance tests around 150 maximum points. Adjust only after a full solo and multiplayer progression run.
