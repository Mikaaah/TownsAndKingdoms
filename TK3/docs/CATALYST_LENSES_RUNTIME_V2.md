# Reusable catalyst lenses · runtime v2

One item, `kubejs:tk3_catalyst_lens`, carries its variant using `minecraft:dyed_color`; charged lenses also carry a custom name. A lens is reusable when used as the catalyst in later mechanism assembly recipes. Blank lenses are assembled with brass sheets and AE2 quartz glass, then charged in the Ars Nouveau Enchanting Apparatus.

| Lens | Display tint | Charge recipe | Mechanism role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Empty Catalyst Lens | `#FFFFFF` | 4 Brass Sheets + AE2 Quartz Glass; Create Mechanical Crafting | Reusable blank lens |
| Netherstar Lens | `#DDF4FF` | Nether Star; Ars Nouveau Enchanting Apparatus, 3,000 source | Wither focus in Tier 7 |
| Everburning Lens | `#FF5A1F` | Ignitium Ingot; Ars Nouveau Enchanting Apparatus, 5,000 source | Ignis focus in Tier 8 |
| Voidguard Lens | `#9A5CFF` | Gauntlet of Guard; Ars Nouveau Enchanting Apparatus, 7,000 source | Ender Guardian focus in Tier 9 |
| Accursed Lens | `#9BCB57` | Cursium Ingot; Ars Nouveau Enchanting Apparatus, 9,000 source | Maledictus focus in Tier 10 |

The Tier 7 Wither, Tier 8 Ignis, Tier 9 Ender Guardian and Tier 10 Maledictus focuses consume their matching colored lens as a reusable catalyst. Exact recipe IDs are `kubejs:tk3/catalysts/empty_lens`, `.../netherstar_lens`, `.../everburning_lens`, `.../voidguard_lens`, and `.../accursed_lens`.
