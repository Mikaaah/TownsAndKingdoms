## ◆ TOWN SQUARE

### Welcome to Towns & Kingdoms

Start here · quest types, controls and your first workshop · **11 QUESTS**

#### [Your kingdom starts here](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-75358291ED74276A)

**◆ GOAL:** Find your main progression route and the supporting guides.

Engineering, magic, exploration and settlement building share one adventure. Chapters I–X are the production spine. The other categories are guides and optional projects you can revisit at any time.

**◇ HOW TO**

1. Read this welcome page.
2. Find the six category headings in the chapter sidebar.
3. Open Chapter I and locate its workshop plan.

**✦ TIP:** Open Mechanical Quests when you want the next machine; open Character Paths when choosing your build.

**Task:** Read and understand this guide · self-reported

#### [Reading the quest board](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-249C1CC92546E539)

**◆ GOAL:** Understand this part of your adventure.

Round item nodes detect an item in your inventory. Guide checkmarks mean you have read the explanation; activity checkmarks mean you have completed the described setup. Large gold milestones unlock the next workshop tier.

**✦ TIP:** Reading guides is safe for the whole team. You can compare all classes without selecting them.

**! WATCH OUT:** A checkmark does not automatically test a farm, choose a class or award skill points.

**Task:** Read and understand this guide · self-reported

#### [Claim your workshop rewards](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-7C1873AD990A9E2E)

**◆ GOAL:** Understand this part of your adventure.

Item goals do not consume your materials. Each chapter milestone awards its named unbreakable finishing tool once per player. Rewards use manual claiming, so revisit completed milestones and collect your own tool.

**◇ HOW TO**

1. Open a completed milestone.
2. Click its reward to claim it.
3. Store your workshop tools separately from adventuring gear.

**✦ TIP:** Put the reward tool into the finishing Deployer; stock replaceable tools while you are still building the chapter.

**Task:** Read and understand this guide · self-reported

#### [Teams and personal choices](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-1E81CE264FFD66B0)

**◆ GOAL:** Understand this part of your adventure.

The campaign uses FTB team quest progress. Workshop stages synchronize with completed milestones when a teammate next logs in. Your skilltree class, subclass and profession investments remain personal.

**✦ TIP:** Agree on who handles crops, processing and exploration, then share the finished supplies.

**! WATCH OUT:** A teammate completing a class guide does not choose that class for you.

**Task:** Read and understand this guide · self-reported

#### [Set up your controls](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-0DDD493E88BF81ED)

**◆ GOAL:** Complete the project described below.

**◇ HOW TO**

1. Check FTB Quests and map controls.
2. Check Epic Fight, ParCool and spell controls.
3. Check the skilltree shortcut.

**✦ TIP:** Search Passive Skill Tree for the class tree. The supplied tree notes O as its intended shortcut, but your actual keybind may differ.

**Task:** Complete this activity · self-reported

#### [Read the actual recipe](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-5040D9B1F28AC74F)

**◆ GOAL:** Complete the project described below.

Use JEI to look up an item’s recipe and uses, then check every process tab. This pack changes important machine and mechanism recipes. Item names alone do not tell you which frame or processing method is required.

**◇ HOW TO**

1. Look up Algal Blend.
2. Compare its manual and mixing routes.
3. Look up the uses of a Kinetic Machine.

**✦ TIP:** JEI bookmarks make a useful shopping list. Keep the casing, mechanism and finishing tool together in your plan.

**Task:** Complete this activity · self-reported

**◇ HOW TO**

### The First Few Days

Shelter, supplies and a workshop that can grow · **6 QUESTS**

#### [Choose a place to begin](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-35A555D8D89382FA)

**◆ GOAL:** Complete the project described below.

Look for access to water, space for crops and a level workshop footprint. Leave room for belts, tanks, a stone generator and later machine rooms rather than fitting every process into your bedroom.

**◇ HOW TO**

1. Set up shelter and a bed.
2. Mark a workshop area.
3. Reserve a separate storage corner.

**✦ TIP:** A workshop next to the farm shortens the first wheat and kelp supply routes.

**Task:** Complete this activity · self-reported

#### [Keep your expedition fed](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-0C0C20356D043CF8)

**◆ GOAL:** Complete the project described below.

Build a steady food supply before traveling far. Farmer’s Delight and the selected cooking mods offer useful meal choices. Check a meal’s tooltip and available ingredients before planning mass production.

**✦ TIP:** Carry one reliable food stack and reserve special meals for longer trips.

**Task:** Complete this activity · self-reported

#### [The first industrial crops](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-528AC548C569B457)

**◆ GOAL:** Complete the project described below.

Wheat and kelp serve both food and machine progression. Clay feeds Algal Blend, and limestone processing can supply bone meal. Keep seeds and replanting stock out of the factory’s consumption buffer.

**◇ HOW TO**

1. Plant wheat.
2. Start a kelp patch in water.
3. Reserve a chest for crops and seeds.

**✦ TIP:** A small reliable farm is more useful than a large farm with nowhere for its harvest to go.

**Task:** Complete this activity · self-reported

#### [Handcraft the starting frame](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-43935EB38B16F0E6)

**◆ GOAL:** Understand this part of your adventure.

Chapter I deliberately starts before automated mechanisms. Seven Andesite Alloy, a wooden slab and Andesite Casing craft the first Kinetic Machine. Use the manual route to obtain the machines that will automate the mechanisms.

**◇ HOW TO**

1. Make Algal Blend and Andesite Alloy.
2. Craft the manual Kinetic Machine.
3. Craft the required starter machines with their frames and matching parts.

**✦ TIP:** Budget several starting frames plus machine parts. The first Press uses an Iron Block; later plate recipes need that Press.

**Task:** Read and understand this guide · self-reported

#### [Give every process an output](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-481FEC28EE03D3D2)

**◆ GOAL:** Complete the project described below.

A chest, hopper or an appropriate collection upgrade turns a working machine into a useful supply line. Decide where items arrive and what should happen when that inventory fills.

**✦ TIP:** Hopper Upgrades transfer to adjacent inventories. Pickup Upgrades collect dropped items; these are different jobs.

**Task:** Complete this activity · self-reported

#### [A working first workshop](https://mikaaah.github.io/TownsAndKingdoms/chapters/#quest-4A6AF6464F3A9F3C)

**◆ GOAL:** Complete the project described below.

Finish a compact area with water power, a press, a mixer and deployers. Leave paths to access filters and replace tools. Continue Chapter I for the exact progression goals.

**✦ TIP:** Label the ingredient buffers so you can return after an expedition and understand your own factory.

**Task:** Complete this activity · self-reported

Seven profession islands support Mining, Logging, Hunting, Exploration, Fishing, Farming and Crafting. The supplied tree permits up to three profession masteries and up to three focus nodes within each profession.
